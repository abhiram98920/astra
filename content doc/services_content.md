## Service Catalog

### 1. Musculoskeletal disorder assessment
- **Description:** Musculoskeletal physiotherapy focuses on treating conditions which affect the muscles, joints, bones, and soft tissues of the body.

### 2. Joint Injections
- **Description:** Relieve pain and inflammation for conditions such as osteoarthritis and tendinitis through targeted medication delivery.

### 3. Shockwave Therapy
- **Description:** A non-invasive treatment using high-intensity sound waves to stimulate healing and reduce chronic pain.

### 4. Acupuncture
- **Description:** Traditional Chinese medicine technique to balance energy flow and treat various physiological conditions.

### 5. Spinal and peripheral joint manual therapy
- **Description:** Hands-on assessment and treatment of joint mobility and function to restore natural movement.

### 6. Urogynecological physiotherapy
- **Description:** Specialist approach to treating pelvic floor dysfunction, bladder, bowel and sexual health issues.

### 7. Pregnancy-related musculoskeletal conditions
- **Description:** Managing physical changes and challenges throughout pregnancy and after childbirth with expert care.

### 8. Post-natal checks
- **Description:** Comprehensive checkups to ensure mother and baby are recovering well after childbirth.

### 9. Men's Health Services
- **Description:** Tailored treatments for erectile dysfunction, pelvic pain, and general male wellness concerns.

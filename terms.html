Terms of Care

Effective from: January 2024

Home / Terms of Care

1. Appointments & Cancellations

We require at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Late cancellations or missed appointments without notice may incur a cancellation fee of up to 50% of the session cost. We reserve the right to charge the full session fee for no-shows.

2. Consent to Treatment

All treatment is provided with the informed consent of the patient. Our clinicians will explain proposed treatments, their risks and benefits before proceeding. Patients may withdraw consent at any time during treatment.

3. Payment Terms

Payment is due at the time of service unless alternative arrangements have been made in advance. We accept card payments, bank transfers, and approved insurance claims. Invoices are available upon request for insurance reimbursement.

4. Patient Responsibilities

Patients are expected to: provide accurate and complete medical history; follow clinical advice and prescribed exercise programmes; attend scheduled appointments; inform us of any changes to their medical condition or medication.

5. Clinical Limitations

Astra Health provides physiotherapy and specialist clinical services. We do not diagnose medical conditions outside our scope of practice. In emergencies, please contact NHS 111 or call 999.

6. Complaints Procedure

We strive for the highest standards of care. If you have a concern, please contact us directly at admin@astrahealth.uk. Formal complaints can be escalated to the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC).

7. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

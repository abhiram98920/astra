Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Home / Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide when booking appointments, completing forms, or contacting us. This includes your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, medical history relevant to your treatment, and billing details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to: schedule and manage your appointments; provide personalised physiotherapy and healthcare treatments; send appointment reminders and follow-up communications; process payments; comply with our legal and regulatory obligations under UK healthcare law (including the Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with: your GP or referring clinician (with your consent); NHS bodies where required; trusted service providers acting on our behalf (bound by confidentiality agreements); regulatory bodies where legally required.

4. Data Retention

Clinical records are retained for a minimum of 8 years from the date of last treatment, or until age 25 for patients treated as minors, in compliance with NHS guidelines. Other data is retained only as long as necessary for its stated purpose.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your personal data; request correction or deletion; restrict or object to processing; data portability; withdraw consent at any time. To exercise your rights, contact us at admin@astrahealth.uk.

6. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to ensure functionality. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You may disable cookies via your browser settings.

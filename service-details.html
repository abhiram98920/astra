What is Shockwave Therapy?

Extracorporeal Shockwave Therapy (ESWT) is a non-invasive, highly effective treatment for chronic pain and various musculoskeletal conditions. It utilizes high-intensity acoustic waves to interact with deep tissues, promoting rapid healing, reducing inflammation, and stimulating cell regeneration.

At Astra Health, we use state-of-the-art radial shockwave devices to target areas such as the shoulders, elbows, knees, and feet, providing significant relief where traditional therapies may have failed.

Key Benefits

  • Non-Surgical

    A safe alternative to surgery with zero downtime.

  • Fast Relief

    Noticeable pain reduction often after the first session.

  • Cellular Healing

    Stimulates collagen production and blood vessel formation.

  • High Success Rate

    Proven efficacy for chronic tendinopathies.
